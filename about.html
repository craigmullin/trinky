The story behind TriNKY

Why TriNKY?

A winding road back to bikes, running shoes, and one rather intimidating third sport.

In 2009, I started watching old Tour de France videos from the library. I relived—for the first time—Lance Armstrong's seven Tour victories with the US Postal Service team. I fell in love with cycling, and after months of obsessive research, I bought a Motobecane Fantom CX cyclocross bike from Bikes Direct.

I loved that bike. It taught me how to ride and how to wrench. A few years later, I sold a record collection to buy my current bike, a 2013 Raleigh Revenio 3.0 with a carbon fork and Shimano 105 components. It's taken me on a century, carried me through winter months on rollers, and accompanied me on countless group and solo rides.

But eventually, I stopped riding more than a couple of times a year.

In 2022, I started running with my wife. Couch-to-5K, a few 5Ks, a half marathon, and then, in 2023, the Flying Pig Marathon in Cincinnati. The one with the thunderstorms.

It was amazing. I missed my five-hour goal by five minutes because I spent five minutes waiting in a porta-potty line, but I had a blast and came away with an enormous sense of accomplishment.

And then, by the end of 2023, I'd stopped running, too.

Cut to 2026.

I'm overweight again. Still. I'm wrestling with some lifelong mental health challenges, and I've become fixated on completing a triathlon. Any distance. Any kind. Anywhere.

I'm 54, and I can feel Father Time gaining on me. So here I am, determined to get back on the bike, start running again, and—oh, please God, no—learn to swim. I've got a lot of work to do, and I'm under no illusion that it'll all go according to plan.

But that's part of the point. I'm not starting from the finish line. I'm starting from here.

And why NKY?

Because Northern Kentucky is home.

For too long, we've been treated as the red-headed stepchild of Greater Cincinnati. No longer!

TriNKY is my idea for bringing together people who want to swim, ride, run, try a triathlon, or simply get out and move with good company. People who are starting over, starting late, starting small, or just starting to wonder what's possible.

If you think a triathlon might be your kind of ridiculous adventure, too, I hope you'll join us.

We'll gather, drink, discuss, train, eat, meet, and laugh along the way.
