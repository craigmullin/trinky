TriNKY is about more than race day. It is about showing up, learning as we go, and finding good company along the way.

This journal will be a place for training notes, local adventures, gear and race lessons, and the small wins that make the bigger goals possible.

You do not need to be fast, experienced, or preparing for a particular race to belong here. Whether you are trying your first swim workout, getting back on the bike, or thinking about a triathlon for the first time, you are welcome.

**Swim. Ride. Run. Together.**
