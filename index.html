The idea

Come as you are.
Find your people.

TriNKY is an idea for a laid-back community of adults who enjoy swimming, cycling, running, or are simply curious about trying a triathlon. No qualifying times, no pressure to keep up, and no experience required. Based in Northern Kentucky and welcoming friends from across the region and beyond.

What we're imagining

Meet up

Talk training, equipment, race plans, and lessons learned over coffee or a beer.

Move together

Casual rides, runs, walks, and perhaps swims as the community takes shape.

Cheer each other on

From a first sprint triathlon to the next big race, every starting line counts.
